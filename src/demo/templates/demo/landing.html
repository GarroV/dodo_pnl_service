{% comment %} Роль, названная в этом абзаце, обязана быть той, у которой набор регистров действительно неполный (T117). До D036 здесь стоял бухгалтер — «an accountant sees only the official ledger», — и после уравнивания доступов абзац стал противоречить списку ролей на пятнадцать строк выше, где у бухгалтера честно перечислены все три регистра. Первое, что читает гость демо, обещало механизм, которого он тут же не увидит. Роль не подставляется из `ROLES` переменной намеренно: предложение объясняет смысл, а не перечисляет данные, и собранное из кусков оно развалилось бы. Согласие с кодом держит `tests/test_demo_landing_claims.py`: он краснеет, если проза назовёт ограниченной роль, у которой все регистры. {% endcomment %}

Switching roles is the point: the unit manager sees one unit and two of the three ledgers, and the numbers on screen are recomputed for what that role may see — the hidden part cannot be recovered by subtracting.

What to look at

  1. Periods — three months: June and July are approved, August is still open.
  2. Payroll sheet — one row per person, one column per component, with a ledger filter.
  3. Trace — open any amount and see which rules produced it, with which inputs and which version of the rate.
  4. Variance report — August against July: a pay rise, a sick leave, a vacation and one manual correction.
  5. Reconciliation — compare the calculation against the accountant's own spreadsheet (below).
  6. Cash expenses — what was spent from the till, unit by unit: add one yourself, edit it, or date it inside a closed month and watch where it lands.
  7. Suppliers — invoices from four counterparties, with the invoice date, the accounting period and the payment date kept apart: one invoice is still unpaid, one is only partly paid, and one line with no expense article yet sits in the classification inbox.
  8. Unallocated — one network-wide amount is still waiting to be split across the units, and the product says so instead of hiding it.
  9. Exports — payout sheet, P&L lines (payroll, cash expenses and supplier invoices side by side) and the accountant's own layout, all as xlsx.
